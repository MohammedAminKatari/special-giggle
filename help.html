How do I update my course schedule?

Open Calendar to review your current classes. For enrollment changes, contact the registrar before the published deadline.

Where can I find academic advising?

Your advisor is listed on your Profile page. You can email the advising office directly at advising@northstar.edu.

Who can help with accessibility support?

Student Accessibility Services can help with accommodations, assistive technology, and confidential planning.
